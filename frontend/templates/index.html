Thesis Monograph & Architecture Specification

Design & Implementation of an Intelligent IoT Air Quality Telemetry and Predictive System

Comprehensive System Architecture, Empirical Methodology, Sensor Calibration, and Deep Learning Performance Evaluation.

1. Abstract & System Context

Rapid global urbanization and industrial expansion have introduced severe atmospheric environmental challenges. Airborne particulate matter ($PM_{2.5}$ and $PM_{10}$) alongside harmful gaseous emissions ($NO_2$, $SO_2$, $CO$) present profound epidemiological hazards to human respiratory and cardiovascular systems. Traditional municipal environmental monitoring infrastructure, while highly precise, suffers from severe spatial sparsity due to astronomical equipment acquisition and maintenance expenditures. This research monograph details the design, hardware synthesis, predictive algorithmic pipeline, and practical software engineering behind Jali Station Kigali—a novel, end-to-end intelligent environmental telemetry system.

Jali Station Kigali bridges the critical gap between hyper-localized sensory hardware and real-time public health decision support. By combining calibrated low-cost Internet of Things (IoT) hardware nodes with advanced deep temporal neural networks—specifically Long Short-Term Memory (LSTM) architectures—the system accurately captures atmospheric dynamics, corrects sensor drift, and provides up to 48 hours of predictive Air Quality Index (AQI) forecasting.

2. Hardware IoT Sensing Matrix & Telemetry

The hardware substrate of the Jali Station Kigali platform relies on distributed multi-sensor edge processing nodes engineered around the 32-bit dual-core Xtensa LX6 ESP32 microcontroller architecture. Each node is equipped with state-of-the-art sensing transducers configured as follows:

  • Particulate Matter Detection (SDS011): Employs laser scattering principles where light diffraction caused by airborne particles is transformed into electrical pulses, accurately quantifying $PM_{2.5}$ and $PM_{10}$ mass concentrations ($\mu g/m^3$).
  • Electrochemical Trace Gas Sensors (MQ Series / MICS-6814): Measures reducing and oxidizing atmospheric gases ($CO$, $NO_2$) via changes in surface electrical conductivity of metal oxide semiconductors.
  • Micro-Meteorological Profiling (BME280): Continuously logs ambient temperature (°C), relative humidity (%), and barometric pressure (hPa) to supply critical covariates necessary for particulate hydrodynamics modeling.

Data transmission utilizes cellular MQTT endpoints and Wi-Fi networks protected with TLS 1.3 encryption, streaming telemetry packets at 10-second intervals to an enterprise-grade InfluxDB time-series database.

3. Mathematical Framework & AQI Calculation Algorithm

The Air Quality Index (AQI) transformation follows the standardized United States Environmental Protection Agency (US-EPA) piecewise linear interpolation formula. For any given pollutant concentration $C_p$, the corresponding sub-index $I_p$ is derived as:

I_p = [ ( I_hi - I_lo ) / ( BP_hi - BP_lo ) ] * ( C_p - BP_lo ) + I_lo

Where:

  • C_p: Truncated measured concentration of pollutant $p$.
  • BP_hi & BP_lo: Breakpoint boundaries corresponding to $C_p$.
  • I_hi & I_lo: Corresponding AQI scale index limits.

The overall system Air Quality Index ($AQI_{total}$) at any given timestamp $t$ is calculated by taking the maximum value among all computed sub-indices across all monitored ambient pollutants:

AQI_total = MAX( I_PM2.5, I_PM10, I_NO2, I_SO2, I_CO )

4. Predictive Machine Learning Architecture

Atmospheric pollution exhibit non-linear time-series behavior heavily dictated by ambient temperature inversions, diurnal vehicle flux, and atmospheric relative humidity. Jali Station Kigali's predictive engine utilizes a hybrid Conv1D + Bi-LSTM (Bidirectional Long Short-Term Memory) neural network trained on over 2.5 million historical telemetry records.

1D Convolutional layers first extract spatial-temporal localized feature maps across sliding historical windows. These features are subsequently passed to two stacked Bi-LSTM layers featuring 128 hidden units each, capturing forward and backward temporal dependencies. A dense linear layer yields the multi-step forecasts ($t+1..t+48$ hours).

5. System Deployment & Environmental Impact

The Jali Station Kigali web architecture is deployed using Docker containerization across cloud infrastructure, consuming low network bandwidth through WebSocket data pushing. By enabling high-resolution real-time visibility into local air quality, municipal stakeholders can deploy targeted traffic throttling during atmospheric inversion alerts, protect vulnerable school populations, and empower citizens with empirical personal health data.
